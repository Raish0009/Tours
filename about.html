About RoyalVistas Tours & Cabs

Building trust through punctuality, courteous police-verified chauffeurs, and authentic Indian travel circuits across Rajasthan, Delhi, Kerala, and Kashmir.

Our Heritage

Two Decades of Excellence in Indian Tourism

Founded in 2001 in Jodhpur, Rajasthan, RoyalVistas Tours & Cabs began with a single mission: to provide dependable, transparent, and dignified road travel across India. Today, we operate a fleet of over 150 sanitized vehicles across New Delhi, Rajasthan, Uttar Pradesh, Kerala, Kashmir, and Maharashtra.

Whether welcoming foreign visitors exploring the Golden Triangle or coordinating large family pilgrimages to Varanasi and Ayodhya, every journey is managed with the timeless Indian spirit of "Atithi Devo Bhava" (Guest is God).
